The Problem

Information isn't the same as understanding.

India's healthcare system produces clinical information abundantly. But patients frequently can't act on it — because it arrives in a language they don't fully read, in terminology they don't understand, and through channels that don't support follow-up.

📊

Capability Gap

Systems produce information — lab reports, prescriptions, dosage schedules. But information overload doesn't help patients who can't interpret it.

🧠

Comprehension Gap

Patients need interpretation. They need clarity. They need actionable guidance — in their own language, at their own literacy level, when it matters.

📱

Engagement Gap

Medical apps retain only 34% of users at 90 days. Manual data logging drives abandonment. Friction kills engagement before it starts.

What PMAS Does

Seven capabilities that connect prescription to adherence.

Each feature maps to a specific failure point in the current patient journey — from counselling through daily medication management to research outcome measurement.

💊

Adherence Tracking

One-tap Taken / Delayed / Missed for each scheduled dose. Daily and weekly adherence rates calculated automatically — the primary outcome variable for research.

🌐

Multilingual Interface

Every patient-facing string translated across English, Telugu, Kannada, Tamil, and Hindi — not just navigation labels, but medication instructions, consent, and safety guidance.

🔔

Local Reminders

In-app toast and browser notification reminders for due doses. Checks every 60 seconds. No cloud service required — works fully offline.

⚠️

Safety Escalation

Redesigned logic that recommends professional evaluation instead of diagnosing. Lists severe symptoms to watch for and provides emergency contact access.

🔬

De-identified Export

Study ID and Study Day replace raw dates. No personal identifiers, medication names, or free-text notes. Structured for HEOR and real-world evidence analysis.

📄

Patient Health Summary

A printable report the patient owns — medications, schedule, adherence summary, symptoms, vitals, upcoming appointments, emergency contact. Separate from research export.

✅

Granular Consent

Six separate acknowledgements — purpose, data collected, voluntary participation, withdrawal right, data usage, and non-substitution for professional care.

🔒

Offline-First Privacy

Data stays on the device. No server transmission. Right to Erasure built in. Designed in alignment with DPDP Act 2023 principles — not a certification claim.

Research Context

A research instrument, not just an app.

PMAS is designed as a research prototype for M.Pharm Pharmacy Practice dissertation work at RIPER, Anantapur — connected to ISPOR India HEOR research and ICMR priorities.

Primary Outcome

Medication Adherence Rate

Measured via PMAS's Taken/Delayed/Missed tracking, validated against MMAS-8 (Morisky Medication Adherence Scale) at baseline and endpoint.

Secondary Outcomes

  • → Medication understanding (pre-post comprehension)
  • → Patient engagement (30/60/90-day retention)
  • → Symptom burden change (pain, BP trends)
  • → Counselling satisfaction
  • → Language preference & comprehension
ICMR-NIRT ISPOR HEOR 2025

The vernacular communication direction was shaped through discussion with Dr. Muniyandi Malaisamy (Scientist E, ICMR-NIRT) at the 5th International HEOR Conference, ISPOR India Andhra Pradesh Chapter. The tool is designed as a research instrument — its de-identified export is structured for HEOR and real-world evidence analysis.
