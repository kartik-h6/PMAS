About PMAS

Bridging the healthcare comprehension gap.

PMAS is a pharmacist-led, multilingual, offline-first medication adherence support prototype that transforms clinical prescriptions into patient-understandable, actionable guidance.

The Origin

PMAS began as a medication scheduling concept before the 5th International HEOR Conference (ISPOR India Andhra Pradesh Chapter). At that conference, a discussion with Dr. Muniyandi Malaisamy (Scientist E, ICMR-NIRT) shaped the project's vernacular communication direction — the idea that healthcare information must be translated not just linguistically, but conceptually, into formats patients can act on.

Dr. Malaisamy's observation that TB awareness programs with significant investment still face engagement challenges reinforced the core thesis: information is not the same as understanding.

Core Thesis

Healthcare suffers from two distinct gaps:

Capability Gap: Systems produce information, but information overload exists without interpretation.

Comprehension Gap: Patients need interpretation, clarity, and actionable guidance — in their own language.

Design Principles

What guides every decision

Earn continuity. Don't engineer dependency.

PMAS should create reasons for patients to stay engaged with their care — never barriers to leaving or seeking a second opinion.

Translate understanding, not just labels.

Multilingual means the counselling explanation changes meaningfully, not just the word "Profile" in five scripts.

Escalate, don't diagnose.

The system recommends professional evaluation and lists symptoms to watch for — it never tells a patient they have an emergency.

Offline-first is a feature, not a limitation.

Local-only storage with no server transmission is the correct architecture for low-connectivity Indian healthcare settings.

Research instrument, not a product.

Every feature maps to a research outcome variable. The de-identified export is structured for HEOR analysis, not for monetisation.
